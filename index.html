How Everything Is Calculated Acquisition Costs (one-time)
DLD (Dubai Land Department) fee: 4% of purchase price
Agent/broker commission: 2% of purchase price
Total acquisition = Purchase Price × 6%

Cash Needed Now
If no downpayment is entered, the property is assumed fully paid.
Cash Now = Downpayment (or full Purchase Price) + Acquisition Costs

Annual Expenses
Service fees = Size (sqft) × Service Fee per sqft/year
Vacancy loss = (Annual Rent ÷ 12) × Vacancy months
Management fees = Annual Rent × Management %
Extras = Extra Annual Fees + Owner Maintenance
Total Annual Expenses = Service fees + Vacancy loss + Management fees + Extras

Net Annual Income
Net Annual Income = Annual Rent − Total Annual Expenses
This is your steady-state yearly profit after all recurring costs, once installments are done.

Installment Period (Post-Handover)
Total installments remaining = Monthly Installment × Remaining Months
Net cashflow during installments = Net Annual Income × (Remaining Months ÷ 12)
Extra cash needed = max(0, Total Installments − Net Cashflow During Installments)
This is the out-of-pocket shortfall if rent doesn't fully cover installments.

Total Cash Invested
Total Cash Invested = Cash Now + Extra Cash Needed
This represents everything you paid out-of-pocket by the end of the installment period.

Gross ROI
Gross ROI = Annual Rent ÷ (Purchase Price + Acquisition Costs) × 100
A quick top-line yield before any expenses.

Net ROI (on cash invested)
Net ROI = Net Annual Income ÷ Total Cash Invested × 100
Your real return on the actual cash you put in. This is the key metric.

Monthly Net (before installment)
Monthly Net Before = Net Annual Income ÷ 12
What you pocket each month in steady state.

Monthly Net (after installment)
Monthly Net After = Monthly Net Before − Monthly Installment
Your actual monthly cashflow while still paying installments. Negative means you're topping up.

Deposits (refundable)
Rent security deposit = Annual Rent × 5% (unfurnished) or 10% (furnished)
DEWA deposit and Chiller deposit are entered directly.
These are shown for info only — they are refundable and not counted in ROI.

Installment Warning
If Downpayment + Total Installments is less than 95% of Purchase Price, a warning appears to help you catch typos. A separate warning shows if monthly cashflow is negative during installments.
